Roast Alarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · App: Roast Alarm (com.roastalarm.app) for Android

In short: Roast Alarm has no user accounts and no servers of its own. Your alarms, settings and mission results stay on your phone. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may receive some device information to serve and measure those ads (see section 4).

1. Who we are

Roast Alarm ("we", "us", "the app") is an alarm clock app for Android. You can contact us at roastalarm1@gmail.com. This email is also our contact point for privacy questions and complaints.

2. Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device. We do not receive it and we cannot see it:

  • Your alarms: times, labels, repeat days, chosen ringtone, roast level and mission settings.
  • Your app settings and your wake-up history and score shown in the Analysis screen.
  • Photos taken for the photo mission (for example a reference photo of a place such as your bathroom sink).
  • Sounds you import yourself (My Sounds).
  • Your unlock state for features such as Roast Nuclear mode or extra mission slots.

Android's backup feature may copy some of this data to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on in your phone settings. We exclude photos and your imported sounds from backup.

3. Permissions and why we ask

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show alarm and snooze notifications.
Exact alarms and full-screen alertsTo ring at the exact time and show the ring screen over your lock screen.
CameraFor the photo mission and the QR/barcode mission. Images are processed on your device.
Physical activity and motion sensorsTo count steps and detect shake or squat movements in missions. Sensor data is not saved or sent anywhere.
Display over other appsTo show the ring screen and open the app straight after an alarm, including over the lock screen.
Do Not Disturb accessSo that alarms can still ring when Do Not Disturb is on. We do not read or change your notifications.
Vibrate, wake lock, start on bootTo vibrate, keep the phone awake while ringing, and set your alarms again after a restart.
Internet and network stateOnly used by the advertising service (section 4).

You can turn off any permission in your phone settings. Some features (for example camera missions) will then stop working.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Roast Alarm is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in our app may collect and share with Google: your IP address (which gives an approximate location), your Android advertising ID, how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance. Google uses this to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Depending on your location and settings, ads may be personalised. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more in Google's page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Some features can be unlocked by watching an optional rewarded ad. Watching is always your choice.

5. Other third-party components

  • Google ML Kit reads QR codes and barcodes on your device. Google's ML Kit components may send basic usage and diagnostic information to Google.
  • The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your contacts, messages, precise location or microphone recordings.

7. How long we keep data and how to delete it

Because your data is on your phone, you control it. You can delete it at any time by deleting individual alarms in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. We do not hold copies on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete remotely. Data held by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

8. Children

Roast Alarm contains humour that can be rude and is meant for adults. The app is intended for users who are 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, please email us.

9. Security

Data stays inside the app's private storage on your phone and is protected by Android's security features. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights (India)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and related rules, you may have the right to know what personal data is processed, to ask for correction or erasure, to withdraw consent, and to complain. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, most of these can be done directly on your phone as described above. If you have a request or complaint, email roastalarm1@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

11. Users outside India

The app is currently offered in India. If it becomes available in other regions, ad consent choices required by local law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) will be shown inside the app.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The latest version is always at this page, and the effective date above tells you when it was last changed.
